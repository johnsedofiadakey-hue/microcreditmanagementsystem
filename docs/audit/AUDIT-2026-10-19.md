## H1 — A bound agent can post a collection for an account outside their assigned route · high

**Where:** `functions/src/commands/collection.ts:26-65`; `functions/src/routes/routes.ts:53-77`; `apps/portal/src/app/(app)/members/[memberId]/page.tsx:299-307`.

**What is wrong:** posting checks that the account is active and in the device's branch, but never checks `route_assignments.agent_pair_id`. The portal says an unassigned account cannot be seen by agents, yet that is only a UI/read-model restriction. **Reproduced:** in a fresh PGlite database, an account with zero route assignments accepted a correctly signed collection and returned `POSTED`.

**Failing scenario:** an agent retains an account ID from a previous route or otherwise obtains a same-branch account ID, collects from that member and posts into their own pair's custody. The assigned pair and supervisor see activity against the wrong route, making cash and visit follow-up harder to reconcile.

**Suggested fix:** check the account's active route assignment against the bound pair inside the posting transaction. Define the policy for commands captured offline *before* an assignment changes, using the captured assignment/version or a supervisor exception path rather than silently reassigning custody. Add tests for unassigned, another pair and reassigned accounts.

## H2 — Collections continue posting into a frozen branch day after close · high

**Where:** `functions/src/commands/collection.ts:26-132`; `functions/src/cash/close.ts:34-53,206-239`; `functions/src/cash/common.ts:22-28`.

**What is wrong:** cash operations call `assertDayOpen`, but collection posting does not. A closed checklist is returned from frozen JSON. **Reproduced:** after a GH₵ 10.00 collection, handover, count and close, a second signed GH₵ 5.00 command returned `POSTED`; custody became 500 pesewas while the closed day's `collectedTodayPesewas` stayed 1000. The existing cash test explicitly expects collections to post after close, so this is a policy gap, not a test accident.

**Failing scenario:** offline commands sync after the manager closes. The member receives a valid receipt, but the closed branch day remains final with an earlier total and no same-day handover or payout can follow. A supervisor reading that close has no explicit late-posting adjustment or exception.

**Suggested fix:** agree a late-arrival policy with the client. Record late postings against a controlled next-day cash obligation or append a visible, independently approved close adjustment/case; preserve the original close and reconcile both days. Show unresolved offline counts before close, test the after-close path, and never discard a valid offline payment silently.

## H3 — The cashier can complete an agent handover without the agent's confirmation · high

**Where:** `functions/src/cash/handovers.ts:25-40,63-102`; `packages/shared/src/cash.ts:18-47`; client experience guide, page 4.

**What is wrong:** `recordHandover` is a single cashier or manager callable. It accepts a pair ID and counted amount, posts the journal and records only `received_by`. There is no agent-started handover, agent acknowledgement, signed phone command or second-party confirmation. The project plan itself lists agent-side handover as missing.

**Failing scenario:** a cashier records less cash than a pair says it handed over. The shortage case exists, but the system has no independent agent attestation of the transfer amount or time. The client's guide promises that both sides record and confirm the handover.

**Suggested fix:** implement an agent-originated handover request/confirmation with an immutable expected amount and pair/device identity, followed by an independent cashier count and confirmation. Differences should remain open as cases; add a two-party end-to-end test before marking Step 6 complete.

## M1 — The portal requests an invalid branch dashboard document path · medium

**Where:** `apps/portal/src/app/(app)/page.tsx:17-56,117-154`; `docs/contracts/API.md` §4; `firestore.rules:90-96`.

**What is wrong:** the overview constructs `dashboards/branches/{branchId}/days/{date}` for a branch (five segments, a collection path), while the contract and rules specify `branchDashboards/{branchId}/days/{date}`. The branch listener will fail when opened. The UI then defaults missing data to zero and states that figures update live, although the Step 7 projection is not built.

**Failing scenario:** a branch manager sees “Live figures unavailable” and zero-value tiles during a real collection day, or could mistake placeholders for operational totals.

**Suggested fix:** use the contracted document path and distinguish unavailable, not-yet-built and genuine zero states. Wire the Step 7 projection before presenting these tiles as live.

## M2 — A single “Total Susu balance” combines accounts from separate businesses · medium

**Where:** `apps/portal/src/app/(app)/members/[memberId]/page.tsx:185,205-208`; `docs/architecture/ADR-001-three-businesses.md:17-28`.

**What is wrong:** the profile sums every account's balance regardless of `businessId`, then labels it as one Susu balance. ADR-001 says each business keeps separate money and every report can be filtered by business.

**Failing scenario:** a member has GH₵ 100 with Gadufia and GH₵ 50 in an OPWS account. Staff see “Total Susu balance GH₵ 150” even though those are separate legal obligations and cannot be paid from one vault.

**Suggested fix:** show one balance per business with its legal name; show a combined figure only if explicitly labelled as an informational total and the client approves it. Make statements and cash views consistently business-specific.

## M3 — Later-stage businesses can receive active Susu products and accounts now · medium

**Where:** `functions/src/members/members.ts:124-146,160-197`; `apps/portal/src/app/(app)/admin/products/page.tsx:92-106`; `functions/test/businesses.test.ts:25-30`; client experience guide, page 7.

**What is wrong:** `createSusuProduct` accepts any active business. All three businesses are seeded `ACTIVE`, and the portal offers all of them. Existing tests create an OPWS “Welfare contribution” as a Susu product and open an account. The guide stages welfare after the Susu pilot, and ADR-001 calls OPWS's first module “Welfare (later)”.

**Failing scenario:** an administrator unintentionally launches a welfare-labelled savings product, collects and permits withdrawals using Susu rules before the welfare policy and module are approved.

**Suggested fix:** decide which contribution products, if any, are in the pilot for each legal entity. Gate product/account activation by an approved business-module rollout setting on the server; mirror it in the portal. Keep the ledger's business separation tests.

## M4 — Lost-trigger recovery can miss an unprocessed command permanently · medium

**Where:** `functions/src/commands/unprocessed.ts:3-49`; `functions/src/triggers/agentCommands.ts:155-170`.

**What is wrong:** the recovery scan is limited by the phone-supplied `capturedAt` string to the last 26 hours. It cannot find a command whose trigger never ran if the phone clock was more than a day behind, or if an outage lasts beyond that window. The source documents this limit.

**Failing scenario:** a signed offline collection reaches Firestore after a long outage with an old captured time, its create trigger is lost, and it has no `status`. The 15-minute sweep never selects it; the agent keeps a pending record and the member gets no ledger credit or receipt.

**Suggested fix:** drive recovery from server create time or a server-maintained pending index, with pagination and alerting for the oldest pending item. Retain `capturedAt` only as field evidence, not the recovery cutoff. Test old-phone-clock and multi-day outage cases.

## M5 — Operational lists stop after the first 200 records · medium

**Where:** `apps/portal/src/lib/api.ts:47-48,103-110`; `apps/portal/src/lib/members-api.ts:72-79,92-102`; `apps/portal/src/app/(app)/members/page.tsx:39-43`; `apps/portal/src/app/(app)/audit/page.tsx:43-47`; `functions/src/cash/reads.ts:8,33-39`.

**What is wrong:** the backend exposes cursors for members, statements and audit events, but the portal does not request the next page. Cash reads have a fixed 200-row limit and no cursor. In-table filtering only searches rows already loaded.

**Failing scenario:** after more than 200 records, a cashier or auditor searches the portal for an older handover or audit event and gets “no match” even though it exists in SQL.

**Suggested fix:** add visible pagination or “load more” to the portal and cursor support to cash reads before real-volume UAT. Make the UI say when a list is partial.

## M6 — Required MFA has no enrolment path or verified enforcement · medium

**Where:** `apps/portal/src/app/sign-in/page.tsx:27-87`; `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md` UI item 1; `docs/ops/ENVIRONMENT_CHECKLIST.md:9-17`.

**What is wrong:** sign-in can answer an MFA challenge, but staff cannot enrol a factor from the portal. The Identity Platform MFA enforcement and App Check settings are unchecked. A clean staff account therefore has no demonstrated path from password setup to the required MFA state.

**Failing scenario:** a newly created cashier either signs in with password only or cannot finish onboarding once MFA is enforced in the console.

**Suggested fix:** add factor enrolment and recovery to the portal, test a new staff account end to end in staging, and record the console enforcement evidence before the pilot.

## M7 — KYC Storage rules and the upload-to-locked-folder journey have no automated coverage · medium

**Where:** `storage.rules:13-29`; `functions/test/rules/firestore.rules.test.ts`; `apps/portal/src/lib/members-api.ts:59-67`; `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md` UI item 3.

**What is wrong:** `test:rules` starts Storage but its tests exercise Firestore only. The portal's KYC upload is also marked untested in the live plan. The rules and server move/sign path protect Ghana Card images, so this is a gap in a sensitive user journey.

**Failing scenario:** a change accidentally permits reading `kyc/{memberId}/` from a client, or denies a valid staff upload. The current 25 emulator tests still pass, while private evidence becomes accessible or onboarding breaks.

**Suggested fix:** add Storage emulator tests for allowed role/path/size/MIME combinations and denied reads, updates and deletes. Run a portal upload → register → move → authorized short-lived view flow in staging with synthetic KYC images.

## L1 — An agent app left open across midnight keeps yesterday's route · low

**Where:** `apps/agent-android/app/src/main/java/com/stormglide/susu/agent/data/RouteRepository.kt:12-31`.

**What is wrong:** the route document date is computed only when the flow starts. The daily route publisher creates a new document at 00:05, but the active listener remains attached to yesterday.

**Failing scenario:** an agent on an early shift sees an old member list until they restart the app. A later route reassignment may not appear on the current day screen.

**Suggested fix:** switch the listener at the Accra date boundary and on foreground resume; keep the offline cached route clearly dated.
