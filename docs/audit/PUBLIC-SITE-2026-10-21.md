# Public website audit — 21 October 2026

**Scope:** Read-only review of <https://gadufia-fiase.web.app/> and `apps/site/`. The site belongs to the backend session. No live enquiry was submitted and no trial data or deployment was changed.

**Method:** Compared the live home/contact pages with source, `docs/contracts/API.md`, `docs/client/SIGN_OFF_PACK.md` and `docs/ops/TRIAL_DEPLOYMENT.md`; inspected all nine public routes in headless Chrome at 320 CSS px, the home page at 390 and 1280 px, and the blank enquiry form's local validation. This is a targeted review, not a full assistive-technology or WCAG conformance certification. The web fetch service could not open the live URL, but direct HTTPS and Chrome loaded it (HTTP 200 on 21 October). ## What is working

- Live pages have clear headings, a skip link, a mobile navigation control, visible focus styling, and reduced-motion rules for the existing entrance/orbit animations.
- The public form validates on both client and server, requires consent, records only an enquiry, has a honeypot and a per-phone daily limit, and does not give the browser direct access to enquiry documents. No money write is exposed through it.
- Micro-credit and welfare detail pages offer enquiries instead of an online account opening action. The calculator discloses that it is an illustration without fees, interest or a payout date.
- No horizontal overflow was observed at 320px on micro-credit, welfare, how-to-join, stay-safe, contact, privacy or accessibility; the home fits 390px.

## Proposed image and motion brief for the site owner

Keep the established field green, warm paper and gold; use the existing terracotta and plum only as restrained accents for STF and welfare. The desktop hero can hold the current short headline and CTAs on the left, with a wide image panel on the right. On phones, put the image first or immediately after the headline, with the CTA always visible above the fold or one short scroll away. Suggested scenes: (1) a trader in an Aflao market setting with a collector, (2) a close view of the registered phone showing a **clearly fictional** receipt preview, and (3) a family or small business moment for the broader brand. Use authentic, consented local photos when available; until then, clearly non-documentary illustrations are safer than synthetic people presented as real customers.

Crossfade and slight scale over roughly 600 ms, with subtle decorative line motion; avoid moving text with each frame. If the slides advance automatically, offer an obvious pause/play control and stop advancing while a control is focused or hovered. Manual previous/next buttons and swipe should work. The first slide must render without JavaScript and without a large layout shift. For `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, keep one static frame and no ambient animation. [W3C carousel guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/) and [pause/stop criterion](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide) support these controls.
